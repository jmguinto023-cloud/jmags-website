Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our service.

Book a Repair
How much does a repair cost?

It depends on the unit's brand, model and fault, so we confirm the price after an assessment. For example, a 55" TV backlight repair starts at ₱8,500, compared with roughly ₱35,000 for a new 55" TV. You get the quote before any work starts.

Do you charge for the assessment?

Assessment terms depend on your location and the unit. Please ask when you book, and we'll tell you upfront.

Do your repairs come with a warranty?

Yes. Every completed repair comes with a written warranty. Its coverage and period are written on your warranty slip.

Do you issue official receipts?

Yes. JMAG ELECTRONIC SERVICE CENTER is BIR-registered (Non-VAT) and issues official receipts, which are useful for school and company procurement.

Which areas do you serve?

Our Cavite branch serves Cavite and most of Batangas. Our San Pablo branch serves Laguna, plus Lipa, Sto. Tomas, Tanauan and Malvar.

What are your business hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

How can I pay?

Payment options are confirmed when we give your quote. Contact us for the methods available for your job.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the fault and whether parts are available. Many repairs can be done during the visit, and we'll give you a time estimate after the assessment.

Do you offer contracts for schools and companies?

Yes. We offer bulk assessments, repair-vs-replace reports and maintenance contracts with clear service terms for schools, banks, hotels, hospitals, malls and offices.

Do you sell TVs and accessories?

We recommend products on our product recommendations, which link to official and Mall stores on Shopee and Lazada.

Have another question? Contact us.
