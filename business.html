Our Story

JMAG's Appliances began as a TV and appliance repair service in San Pablo City, Laguna. Hands-on repair work taught us that many units people were about to throw away could be fixed for a fraction of the price of a new one. That idea became our motto: Repair first. Save more. Today we serve households, schools and companies from two branches, Cavite and San Pablo.

How We Work

  1. Inquiry. Message us on Facebook, call, email or use our inquiry form. Tell us the brand, size and problem.
  2. Schedule. We set a home or on-site visit during our hours, Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
  3. Assessment. Our technician checks the unit and finds the fault.
  4. Quote. You get the repair cost before any work starts. For organizations, we can prepare a repair-vs-replace report.
  5. Repair & testing. We repair the unit and test it with you.
  6. Warranty & official receipt. You receive a written warranty and an official receipt.

Warranty

Every completed repair comes with a written warranty. The coverage and period are written on your warranty slip and depend on the repair done.
