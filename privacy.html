Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information.

JMAG ELECTRONIC SERVICE CENTER ("JMAG's Appliances", "we") respects your privacy and follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

Information We Collect

When you contact us, we collect only what you choose to give us: your name, organization, contact number, email, service address and details about your unit.

How We Use It

  • To answer inquiries, schedule visits and give quotes
  • To carry out repairs and honour warranties
  • To issue official receipts and meet legal and tax obligations

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when the law requires it or when it's needed to deliver your service.

Storage & Security

We keep your information only as long as we need it for these purposes or as the law requires, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your Rights

Under RA 10173, you have the right to be informed and to access, correct, object to, erase or block your personal data, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Contact

For privacy concerns, email jmagsappliances@gmail.com or call 0938 636 9374.
