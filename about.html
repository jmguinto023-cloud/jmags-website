Who We Are

JMAG's Appliances is the trade name of JMAG ELECTRONIC SERVICE CENTER, a TV and appliance repair business registered with the DTI (Business Name No. 2212238) and the BIR (Non-VAT). We started in San Pablo City, Laguna, and we now run a second branch serving Cavite.

What We Believe

Repair first. Save more. Many TVs and appliances that look "dead" can be brought back with the right repair, such as a backlight replacement, board repair or panel work, for much less than a new unit. We assess honestly and tell you if a repair makes sense.

Who We Serve

  • Households, through home service repair
  • Schools, through maintenance programs
  • Banks, hotels, hospitals, malls and offices, through corporate maintenance

Our Commitments

  • Clear assessment before any repair
  • Written warranty on completed repairs
  • Official receipt for every job
