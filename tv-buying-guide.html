LED vs QLED vs OLED: what are you paying for?

LED TV usually means an LCD television lit by LEDs. QLED is also an LCD-based television with a quantum-dot layer that can improve color, while OLED uses self-emitting pixels for particularly deep blacks. Picture quality also depends on the actual model, panel, processing and lighting conditions.

What TV size should I buy?

Measure your wall or cabinet first. Check the TV's full width, leg spacing, VESA mount pattern and how far you sit. A 43-inch TV is a common starting point for a smaller lounge; 55-inch and larger models suit many bigger rooms. Actual comfort depends on viewing distance, resolution and personal preference.

Full HD or 4K Smart TV?

4K UHD has 3840 × 2160 pixels; standard Full HD has 1920 × 1080. For larger screens and 4K content, a true 4K panel can look sharper. Check the exact screen size and variant because sellers sometimes put different resolutions in the same listing.

JMAGS TV shopping checklist

  1. Confirm the exact model number and screen size on the official listing.
  2. Read the seller's warranty and service-center terms, not just the promotional banner.
  3. Check HDMI ports, Wi-Fi bands, app compatibility and whether wall mounting needs extra parts.
  4. Compare delivery, installation, return policies and reviews for your selected variant.
  5. Use suitable surge protection or an AVR if your local supply is unstable, based on the television maker's guidance.
Technician tip: If your existing TV has sound but a black screen, don't immediately buy a new one. A failed backlight is one possible cause; ask a qualified technician to diagnose it first.

No. QLED refers to a display technology, not the resolution. Verify whether the specific model is Full HD or 4K.
